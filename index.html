                Questions

                Where is my data stored?
                In your browser's storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server and there's no account. Clearing your browser data removes it, so export a CSV or keep a share link if you want a backup.
                How does the share link work?
                The group is compressed and written into the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Whoever opens it sees a preview of the people, expenses and total, then chooses whether to import it as a new group. Their copy is separate from yours.
                How does an itemised receipt split tax and tip?
                Add each line from the bill and tap who had it. Shared lines are split evenly between the people on them. Tax and tip are worked out on the subtotal, then shared in proportion to what each person ordered, so someone who had a $15 noodle dish pays less service than someone who had the $38 snapper.
                Is it really the fewest payments?
                Yes, for groups with up to 16 people who have a balance. Split It finds every set of people whose balances cancel out and settles each set on its own, which is what makes the plan as short as it can be. Larger groups use a faster method that still never needs more payments than people minus one.
                What happens to leftover cents?
                When an amount doesn't divide evenly, the extra cents go one at a time to people in the order they were added. $10 between three people is $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33, so the shares always add up to the exact total.
                Can I mix currencies in one group?
                Yes. Each expense has its own currency, and balances are kept separately for each one. A currency switch appears above the totals as soon as a group uses more than one.
